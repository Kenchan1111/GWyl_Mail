🎓 ARCHITECTURE

Message Email → Canonicalisation → Hash SHA-256
                                      ↓
                    ┌─────────────────┴─────────────────┐
                    ↓                                   ↓
              Sigstore (immédiat)               OTS (différé)
              - OIDC Identity                   - Bitcoin blockchain
              - Rekor timestamp                 - Legal proof
              - Trust: MEDIUM                   - Trust: HIGH
                    ↓                                   ↓
                    └─────────────────┬─────────────────┘
                                      ↓
                              Dual Proof (JSON)
                              - Signature DSSE
                              - Policy hash
                              - Anti-replay
                              - Privacy minimal
